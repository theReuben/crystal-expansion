# Credits

Crystal Expansion is a recombination of other people's work. Almost nothing in
this repository originates here. This file is the attribution; please keep it
intact in any fork, and reproduce it in any distribution.

**This project is not official Pokémon CrystalDust, and is not affiliated with
or endorsed by the CrystalDust team.** It is an unofficial port of their content
onto a different engine base. Any bugs you find here are almost certainly ours,
not theirs — please do not report Crystal Expansion issues to the CrystalDust
developers.

---

## Pokémon CrystalDust

The Crystal content — Johto's maps and scripts, the Pokégear, the radio, the
phone, apricorns and Kurt, the bug-catching contest, the day/night system, Mom's
bank, the GBS sound engine work, and the enormous body of text and art that
makes it Crystal — comes from
[**Pokémon CrystalDust**](https://github.com/Deokishisu/CrystalDust).

- **Sierraffinity** — project lead and principal developer
- **Deokishisu** — project lead, maps, scripting, and repository maintenance
- **Alpha testers**, from the project's `alpha_testers.txt`: Worcestershirey,
  axipher, MrAPT1, Furret, Alpha Remaster, PlatinumMaster
- and the wider CrystalDust contributor and translator community

CrystalDust ships no `CREDITS` file of its own, and its in-game credits sequence
is still Emerald's unmodified staff roll, so this list is assembled from the
repository's README and `alpha_testers.txt`. **It is very likely incomplete.**
If you contributed to CrystalDust and are missing here, that is an error on our
part and not a slight — please open an issue and it will be fixed.

CrystalDust links: [DoMoreAwesome](https://domoreaweso.me) ·
[Discord](https://discord.gg/gC6mEQB)

## RHH's pokeemerald-expansion

The engine — modern battle mechanics, the full move/ability/species roster,
follower Pokémon, the debug menu, and the build system — is
[**pokeemerald-expansion**](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion)
by **RHH (Rom Hacking Hideout)**, pinned at version 1.17.0.

```
Based off RHH's pokeemerald-expansion 1.17.0 https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion/
```

The expansion's own contributor list — several hundred people — is preserved
verbatim at
[`docs/port/upstream-CREDITS-expansion.md`](docs/port/upstream-CREDITS-expansion.md).

## pret

Neither of the above could exist without [**pret**](https://github.com/pret),
whose [`pokeemerald`](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald) decompilation is the
common ancestor of both projects, and whose
[`pokecrystal`](https://github.com/pret/pokecrystal) is the reference for
Crystal's original behaviour.
